# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

Waveframe is a free open-source hobby project. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Waveframe application does and does not intentionally collect.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Waveframe does not intentionally collect or store personal information through the game.**

You do not need to create an account or provide an email address, real name, or other personal information to play.

## 2. Camera Data

Waveframe uses your device's camera for hand tracking and gesture recognition.

When you grant camera permission:

- Camera frames are processed locally in your browser.
- Waveframe does not intentionally upload camera frames to its servers.
- Camera frames are not intentionally recorded or stored by Waveframe.
- Camera access is used only for the game's hand-tracking functionality.
- Camera access ends when the game no longer has permission to use your camera.

The camera is accessed through your browser's normal permission system, and you can revoke permission at any time through your browser settings.

## 3. Local Game Data

Waveframe may store gameplay information in your browser's local storage, including:

- Optional player name
- Game scores
- High scores
- Streak records
- Timestamps associated with game records

This information is stored locally by your browser and is not intentionally transmitted to Waveframe.

You can remove this information by:

1. Using Waveframe's **Clear Data** option, if available;
2. Clearing the site's local storage; or
3. Clearing the site's browser data.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

Waveframe does not intentionally use analytics services, advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or cookies for user tracking.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use:

- **MediaPipe** by Google: For hand tracking and gesture recognition. Runs entirely in your browser and processes camera data locally on your device. [MediaPipe Privacy](https://developers.google.com/mediapipe)
- **Vercel**: For hosting the static website. No server-side processing occurs.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Waveframe does not intentionally collect personal information from users, including children.

Because the game does not require an account or submission of personal information, Waveframe does not maintain user profiles or accounts.

## 7. Data Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored locally in your browser rather than on Waveframe's servers, you can delete it yourself at any time by clearing the game's local storage or browser site data.

There is no Waveframe account or server-side player profile that needs to be deleted.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date will be updated accordingly.

## 9. Contact

Waveframe is a hobby project. If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or the project's data practices, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.

---

**In short:** Waveframe is designed to process gameplay and camera input locally in your browser. It does not intentionally collect or store your camera feed or personal information.
